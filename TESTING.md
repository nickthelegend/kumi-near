Sandbox tests (each assertion is a real transaction or view on the sandbox chain):

1. `crew_dao_is_created_by_factory_with_member_policy`: the factory creates the DAO with the hub's policy (3-member role, bond 0). An uninvited join is rejected. A bad slug and a short deposit are rejected. A taken slug fails in the factory, the deposit comes back, and no crew is added.
2. `share_math_join_at_nav_and_exact_ragequit`: proportional shares. A later join is minted at exactly `amount × supply / NAV`, using the pool marks read on-chain. Ragequit pays exact pro-rata NEAR and SOL. The last exits leave zero dust, and a double exit fails.
3. `proposal_vote_execute_and_vote_replay_rejected`: the DAO FunctionCall proposal is filed. A direct `execute_trade` is rejected (`ERR_DAO_ONLY`) and an outsider can't vote. 1 of 3 approvals leaves it voting; 2 of 3 executes 20% of NEAR. **Vote replay**: a second DAO proposal for the same trade is approved but ends `Failed`, and state is unchanged. A second voted trade (a sell) executes, and the receipts are in order.
4. `captain_key_limits_are_enforced_and_it_cannot_withdraw`: the vote installs a function-call key limited to `captain_trade`. **Over the size limit, a wrong asset and the daily limit are each rejected.** The key can't call `ragequit`, `add_asset` or `claim_token`, or transfer NEAR. Another signer is `ERR_NOT_CAPTAIN`. Revoking the captain by vote deletes the key.
5. `ragequit_during_pending_trade_is_exact_and_trade_uses_what_is_left`: a member leaves mid-vote with exactly 1/3 and drops out of the DAO role. The two members left pass it 2 of 2, and it executes on 50% of the 6 NEAR left, not the 9 NEAR before the exit.
6. `follower_mirrors_pro_rata_and_pays_fee_above_high_water_mark`: crew and vault fill in one swap at the same price. The fee is exactly 20% of the gain above the HWM, paid in kind to the crew, and the HWM moves. A repeat crystallize pays nothing. The follower exits with the exact vault slice.
7. `front_running_the_crew_is_bounded_by_the_attached_quote`: a follower buys ahead of an open vote and moves the price. The crew's fill would be worse than the quote minus 3%, so the pool refuses, holdings are restored exactly, and the hub isn't left busy.
8. `ragequitter_loses_the_dao_vote_and_a_stale_approval_cannot_carry_a_trade`: new crews give the hub's DAO role exactly `call:AddProposal` plus `remove_member_from_role:AddProposal/VoteApprove`. Bob is captain and approves an open trade, then ragequits everything: `member_removed` is logged, the DAO role and the hub's member list are alice and cy, his captain key is deleted and a `captain_trade` with it fails. **Bob's vote on a new trade is refused (`ERR_PERMISSION_DENIED`).** With two members left, 1 approval leaves a trade voting and 2 execute it. **Bob's earlier approval plus alice's reach the DAO's 2 of 2, but the hub's recount sees 1 of 2 shareholders: `ERR_STALE_VOTES`, the proposal ends Failed, and holdings are unchanged.** Bob can't rejoin uninvited; `remove_ex_member` refuses a shareholder and a non-member.
9. `a_failed_vote_removal_is_recorded_loudly_and_retried`: the crew takes the hub's removal permission away, then bob ragequits. He is paid; `member_removal_failed` and "retry with remove_ex_member" are logged; `get_stale_voters` lists him; he still sits in the DAO role. His vote after leaving plus alice's pass the DAO but the hub refuses (1 of 2 shareholders). A retry without the permission fails loudly again. After the crew restores it, anyone's retry removes him, and 2 of 2 executes the next trade.
10. `upgrade_from_the_nov9_hub_removes_a_legacy_ragequitter`: deploys the hub bytes that ran on testnet on Nov 9; bob ragequits and keeps his DAO vote (the bug, reproduced). The new code is deployed over it and the state reads back unchanged. The recount already ignores bob's vote. `remove_ex_member` needs his ragequit receipt (none, or a join receipt, is refused); under the old DAO policy it fails loudly and is kept as pending; after the crew grants the permission, the retry removes him. This is the path used on testnet on Nov 10.
